Siva Prasad Vajja — Java/Spring Boot/React/Telecom Product Engineer

Purpose: This document is a complete study roadmap and research brief to give to Codex.
The goal is to prepare for a move from 6D Technologies into product companies only — especially telecom/BSS products, B2B SaaS, enterprise platforms, FinTech/product platforms, and AI-enabled SaaS.

Important constraint: Do NOT include service/consulting companies such as TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Accenture, Capgemini, HCLTech, Tech Mahindra, LTIMindtree, Persistent, Deloitte Consulting, EY, PwC, KPMG, etc. They are outside this target strategy.
