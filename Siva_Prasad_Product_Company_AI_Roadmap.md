# 53. Rules for the Codex Research

1. Use official documentation whenever possible.
2. For current company information, verify with current web sources.
3. Do not rely on old salary or job information without clearly labeling it.
4. Distinguish product companies from service/consulting companies.
5. Do not invent technologies used by a company.
6. Clearly label assumptions.
7. Prefer current 2026 information for company/job-market research.
8. For frameworks, prefer official documentation.
9. Explain concepts deeply enough for a 5+ year engineer.
10. Include diagrams using Mermaid where useful.
11. Use Java examples for backend/AI topics where possible.
12. Don't replace Java with Python unnecessarily.
13. Connect every new technology to real product engineering.
14. Explain trade-offs.
15. Include interview questions after every major topic.
16. Include senior-level "why" questions, not just definitions.
17. Include production failure scenarios.
18. Include practical exercises.
19. Track progress using checkboxes.
20. Keep the roadmap focused on product engineering.
