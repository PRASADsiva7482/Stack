## 4. How to research one company correctly

1. Open the official product page and the exact official job posting.
2. Record what is explicitly stated, with date and URL.
3. Separate product engineering from implementation, customer support, and consulting roles.
4. Search the posting for Java version, Spring, database, messaging, cloud, testing, on-call, and design responsibilities.
5. Use employee/interview reports only as anecdotal signals, never as guaranteed process.
6. Do not put salary ranges into the roadmap unless a reliable, current source states scope, location, level, and total-compensation basis.
7. Build a role-specific gap list and adapt the preparation order.

## 5. Domain questions to ask in screening

- Is this role core product engineering, customer implementation, or a hybrid?
- What component will I own and who operates it in production?
- What are the main reliability, latency, data, and compliance constraints?
- Which Java/Spring and database versions are in the service?
- Are Kafka/Redis/cloud/Kubernetes required production ownership or useful exposure?
- How are design reviews, on-call, incident response, and release rollbacks handled?
- How is product impact measured?

## 6. Interview preparation by target lane

### Telecom/BSS

Practice subscriber lifecycle, product catalog, order decomposition, provisioning, mediation, rating, charging, billing, dispute, reconciliation, workflow, and eventual consistency. Connect Camunda and batch experience carefully.

### SaaS/CRM

Practice multi-tenant authorization, tenant-aware caching/events, noisy-neighbor control, search, audit, API evolution, usage metering, and customer-impact metrics.

### Payments

Practice idempotency keys, payment state machines, immutable ledger entries, reconciliation, retries, duplicate callbacks, fraud boundaries, and exactly-once-effect design.

### AI-enabled products

Practice RAG ingestion/retrieval, access control, tool authorization, evaluation, prompt injection, cost/latency, human approval, and honest project evidence.
