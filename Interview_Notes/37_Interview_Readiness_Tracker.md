# Interview Readiness Tracker

This is the execution plan for the roadmap. The notes are reference material; readiness comes from timed practice, correction, and demonstrable evidence.

## 1. Definition of ready

You are ready for a target role when you can:

- Explain every resume line without exaggeration.
- Solve common medium coding problems within 35 minutes and test them aloud.
- Complete a small LLD or machine-coding exercise within 60-75 minutes.
- Lead a system-design discussion for 45-60 minutes with assumptions, scale, failure handling, and trade-offs.
- Diagnose common production symptoms from evidence rather than guessing.
- Explain Java/Spring/JPA/SQL deeply enough to answer follow-ups.
- Discuss Kafka, Redis, cloud, security, and AI at the level you actually possess: production, exposure, lab, or design.
- Tell six to ten concise behavioral stories.
- Maintain a consistent score of at least 3/4 in two consecutive mock loops.

## 3. Weekly cadence for two hours per day

| Day | Work |
|---|---|
| Monday | 45 min concept study, 45 min coding, 30 min spoken questions |
| Tuesday | 30 min review, 60 min coding, 30 min correction log |
| Wednesday | 45 min backend/data study, 45 min design, 30 min behavioral story |
| Thursday | 30 min concept review, 60 min coding or machine coding, 30 min tests |
| Friday | 30 min production scenario, 45 min question bank, 45 min resume/project deep dive |
| Saturday | One timed mock section, correction, and one portfolio/lab increment |
| Sunday | Re-test weak areas, update scores, plan the next week; keep the workload lighter |

If you have only one hour, use 25 minutes coding, 20 minutes one technical topic, and 15 minutes speaking. Speaking practice is not optional: interviews test retrieval and communication under time pressure.

## 11. Final pre-interview checklist

- Resume and LinkedIn use the same verified story.
- Every technology is classified as production, exposure, lab, or design exercise.
- Intro is under 90 seconds.
- Two project deep dives and six behavioral stories are rehearsed.
- At least 20 coding problems were re-tested, not merely read.
- One LLD and one system design were completed under time.
- Production incident answers include evidence, mitigation, root cause, and prevention.
- AI answers include security, evaluation, cost, and human approval for side effects.
- Notice period, location, compensation expectations, and role preferences are prepared honestly.
- Questions for the interviewer are ready.
