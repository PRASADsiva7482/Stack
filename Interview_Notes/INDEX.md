- `NOT STARTED`: no personal evidence yet.
- `IN PROGRESS`: actively studying or building.
- `REVISED`: can explain and has corrected mistakes.
- `INTERVIEW READY`: timed answers/coding/design pass self-checks.
- `PRODUCTION READY`: working lab/project includes tests, failure handling, observability, and trade-offs.
